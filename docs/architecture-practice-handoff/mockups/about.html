I have been an architect of one kind or another for more than a decade.

First of applications, as a lead architect at Appirio. Then of the front-office systems of large enterprises, during nine years at McKinsey. Now of whole ventures.

The object has changed. The work still asks the same question: how do the parts fit, and who is accountable for the whole?

The technical seat

I went in for two weeks. I stayed as CTO.

At McKinsey, the work that shaped me most was serving as CTO of North America's first large-scale digital business build: a new company stood up from scratch next to an industrial parent.

I also spent two years as lead faculty for the firm's experienced-hire program. That taught me that a design is only finished when someone else can run it.
