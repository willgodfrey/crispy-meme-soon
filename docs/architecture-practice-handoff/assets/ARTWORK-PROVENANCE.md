## Original assembly prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: bespoke photographic art for the homepage of independent venture architect Will Godfrey, expressing how commercial, technical and institutional layers fit together. Create a refined physical architectural assembly sculpture photographed in an art director's studio, not a building or city. One large beautifully composed assembly of three thin folded planes and interlocking structural slabs: a near-black anodized aluminum vertical folded frame, a milky translucent glass horizontal plane, and a smaller deep wine/burgundy stone rectangular upright. Precise clean joinery, grounded and plausible, not floating, architectural maquette with a long slotted central space and open cantilever. Entire sculpture visible, dramatically sculptural but simple and meaningful, asymmetric three-quarter front perspective. Cool pale chalk grey cyclorama floor/background, long soft directional afternoon shadows to left, tactile museum object quality, extremely high end architecture journal still life, medium format photography, restrained dark materials and translucent edges. Composition: wide 3:2 landscape, sculpture occupies right 65 percent and center of image with substantial plain pale background on left and enough breathing room around all edges. No people, no typography, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no office objects, no computer, no skyscrapers, no spheres, no generic AI gradients. This is concept artwork, not a depiction of a real client project.
